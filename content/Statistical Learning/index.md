Lecture notes for **UW-Madison STAT615, Spring 2026**, organized into four chapters.

1. [[1 Prediction and Consistency|Prediction and consistency]] — Bayes classifiers, local averaging, concentration inequalities, and consistency. Lectures 0-9.
2. [[2 Empirical Risk and Generalization|Empirical risk and generalization]] — ERM, uniform convergence, VC dimension, and Rademacher complexity. Lectures 9-13.
3. [[3 Linear Classifiers and Support Vector Machines|Linear classifiers and support vector machines]] — LDA, QDA, logistic regression, margins, and duality. Lectures 13-17.
4. [[4 Kernels Gaussian Processes and Conformal Prediction|Kernels, Gaussian processes, and conformal prediction]] — RKHS, kernel regression, spectral expansions, and prediction sets. Lectures 17-23.
